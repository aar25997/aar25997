Our Standards

Quality Is Not a Trend. It's Our Foundation.

These are the commitments behind every plate we serve, and the way we'll hold ourselves accountable to them.

  1. I

    Intentional ingredient sourcing

    We choose every ingredient on purpose. We look for suppliers whose practices we understand and trust, and we would rather pay more for something good than serve something we can't stand behind.

  2. II

    Thoughtful cooking fats

    We are building our kitchen around avoiding industrial seed oils wherever our preparation allows. As our recipes and suppliers are finalized, we'll publish exactly what we cook with.

  3. III

    Ethically raised meats

    We seek out meats raised with care. When a specific claim applies to a product we buy, such as grass-fed beef, we'll say so on the menu. When it doesn't, we won't.

  4. IV

    Traditional techniques, carefully developed recipes

    Shawarma roasted slowly on the spit. Falafel made the way Egyptian kitchens have made it for generations. Recipes tested and refined until they are right, not just fast.

  5. V

    Fresh preparation

    Our sauces, our falafel and our proteins are prepared in our own kitchen, throughout the day.

  6. VI

    Honest transparency

    Ask us anything about what's in your food. We'll tell you, and if we don't know yet, we'll find out. Ingredient and allergen details will be published with our final menu.

  7. VII

    Genuine hospitality

    Great food is only half of it. We want every guest to feel welcomed, looked after and glad they came.

Questions about our food?

We're happy to talk about sourcing, preparation or allergens.
